Privacy

This is a plain-English summary of what happens to your information on this copy of SellTrack.

What's stored

  • Your username and a scrambled version of your password. Nobody can read your password.
  • What you enter: products, sales, customer names, costs, payments, and the names of people in your split.
  • Your recent sign-ins, including the address and device they came from, so you can spot one that wasn't you.
  • A record of security-related actions, such as password changes, to help investigate problems.

What isn't

  • SellTrack doesn't ask for your email address or real name.
  • There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and nothing is loaded from other companies' sites.

Who can see it

Anyone who logs in to your account can see everything in it. The site's admin can see your username and account activity, but the admin pages don't show your sales, products or split.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you logged in and protects your forms. It isn't used for anything else.

Deleting your information

You can permanently delete your account under Settings. A record of security-related actions is kept, without being linked to your account. Backups are replaced over time.
