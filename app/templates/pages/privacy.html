Privacy

This is a plain-English summary of what happens to your information on this copy of SellTrack.

What's stored

  • Your username and a scrambled version of your password. Nobody can read your password.
  • What you enter: products, sales, customer names, costs, payments, and the names of people in your split.
  • Your recent sign-ins, including the address and device they came from, so you can spot one that wasn't you.
  • A record of security-related actions, such as password changes, to help investigate problems. It holds no customer names, no names of people in your split, and no amounts of money.
  • Messages you send to support, and the replies.

What isn't

  • SellTrack doesn't ask for your email address or real name.
  • There are no advertising or tracking cookies, and nothing is loaded from other companies' sites.

Who can see it

Anyone who logs in to your account can see everything in it. The site's admin can see your username, your account activity, how many records your account has, and anything you send to support. The admin pages don't show your sales, products or split.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you logged in and protects your forms. It isn't used for anything else.

How long it's kept

  • A sale you delete is permanently removed after 30 days.
  • Sign-ins older than 90 days are removed, apart from your latest 20.
  • Notifications you've read are removed after 180 days.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it.

Getting a copy, and deleting your information

You can download everything in your account, or permanently delete your account, under Settings. A record of security-related actions is kept, without being linked to your account. Backups are replaced over time.
