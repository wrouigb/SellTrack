# Using SellTrack

SellTrack keeps track of what you sell, shows what you're making, and works out who owes whom when you run a business with other people.

## Getting started

1. **Register** with a username and password. There's no email address.
2. **Save your recovery codes.** You only see them once. With no email, they're how you get back in if you forget your password. Keep them somewhere other than the device you're using.
3. **Follow the first steps:** your time zone, who shares the profit, your first product, and your first sale. You can skip any of these.

Partners can share one login. Everything in an account is shared by whoever logs in to it, and people in the split are just names.

## Sales and products

Add a **product** with what one costs you, and its usual selling price if it has one. When you add a **sale**, the price fills in, and you can change it. Each sale keeps the cost at the time, so changing a product's cost later doesn't change old profit figures.

On the **Sales** page you can search, filter by date, product or who received the money, and sort by any column. You can delete several at once, and undo that straight after. **Download CSV** gives you whatever the filters are showing.

## The dashboard

**Overview** shows your money in and profit for the period you choose, compared with the period before, along with a chart, your best products, your latest sales and who owes whom. While a month is still going, it's compared with the same days of the month before.

## The split

The split works from real money: what came in, less what people spent, since your last settle-up.

1. Add your **people** and their shares, which need to total 100%. Someone usually receives the sale money, and new sales go to them unless you say otherwise.
2. Log **upfront costs**, such as stock you buy, a stall fee or packaging. Whoever paid gets it back first.
3. Log **payments** when someone hands over money, such as part-paying a debt.
4. **Overview** shows who owes whom, with the fewest payments that make everyone square.
5. When you've paid each other, **settle up**. That saves the figures to your history and starts the tally again from $0.

**Why doesn't the split match the profit on my sales?** Profit on sales uses each product's cost per item. The split uses money actually spent. So buying $100 of stock counts in the split straight away, and in profit on sales only as each item sells.

**Made a mistake?** You can undo the most recent settle-up, which also puts shares back as they were. Once a sale, cost or payment has been settled, its money is locked until you undo that settle-up. Its date, name and notes can still change.

**Someone leaving?** Settle up, then archive them under People. Their history stays.

## Your account

Under **Settings**:

- **Security:** change your password, make new recovery codes, see recent sign-ins, and log out every device. Check the sign-ins now and then if you share a login.
- **Preferences:** your time zone and light or dark mode.
- **Your data:** download everything as a zip of spreadsheet files, or permanently delete your account.

**Forgot your password?** Use a recovery code on the login page's recovery link. Each code works once. If you've lost those too, the site's admin can give you a temporary password.

## Notifications and support

The **bell** shows anything new: a sign-in from somewhere new, settle-ups, replies from support and announcements.

To ask for help, use the form on the **Help** page or under **Support** in the account menu. Replies appear in your notifications and on the Support page.

## How long things are kept

A sale you delete is permanently removed after 30 days. Sign-ins older than 90 days are removed, apart from your latest 20. Notifications you've read are removed after 180 days. Everything else stays until you delete it. The [privacy page](/privacy) has the details.
